In addition to cutting out nearly all content from the main version of Outlaws aside from its ten factions, some components for those factions have been excluded or significantly altered as well. This file lists all of the most significant general modifiers and components for the Outlaws factions that were removed and any additional adjustments to their abilities and components that are unique to this mod, along with the list of new and adjusted components for Redux factions that were kept.

#### Excluded Modifiers (and all support for them):
- City Defense
- Russian Unifier

#### Excluded Components:
Deseret:
- Blue Castle Reactor
- Deseret Data Center

Camelot Foundation:
- Pridwen-class

All Russian Factions: (no, Crimson Line doesn't get to keep the units even though they originally belonged to that faction)
- RCB Conscript
- RCB Infantry
- RCB Marksman

Aurelian Group:
- Outback Station

Venice Beach CS:
- Seaside Resort

#### Preserved New Components

#### Preserved Component Adjustments
Cult of Ignis:
- Plantation (still belongs to Deadrock Clan)

Hexlock:
- Heat Plant (still becomes a standalone building instead of faction exclusive Hydro Plant replacement; however, it only gives 2 units of Power now)

New Afrika:
- Revolutionary Council

Proklyatyye:
- Troika (Politburo in Outlaws, formerly Gulag)
- Verkhovny Sovet (formerly Five-Year Plan)

#### Other Adjustments:
- Church of the Water Tsaritsa is now The Harbingers' favored religion
- Continental Generals are now expended when creating Citadels; however, they start with free XP when spawned from the third era onward
- Cut Away the Self II now generates 2 Culture per turn
- Mission to Isolationists is now a standalone national wonder and no longer gives a free religious building in its city
    - Deseret can no longer build Ally with Isolationists or Annex Isolationists
- Mounted and Mounted Ranged are no longer separate unit types
- Neutron Bombs are now Missile units (Atomic Bombers do not exist in this mod)
- Nowa Polska's unit training speed boost at the onset of defensive wars increased to 50%
- Several UUs have been weakened to reflect the lack of mid/late-game unit strength rebalancing
- Siege Nukes do not exist in this mod
- Some adjustments to Personality Values have been made to account for the absence of policy rebalancing
- Stone of Ages' base Culture yield reduced to 1, but it once again gives the capital a free Riding Arena
- Vodka Brewery now exclusively belongs to The Harbingers
